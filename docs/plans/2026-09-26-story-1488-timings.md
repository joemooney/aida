## Reading (for the slice gates)

- **The lib.rs penalty on edit-rebuild is ~2.9s (20.0 vs 17.1 leaf), ~15%.**
  The ~17s floor is crate-wide front-end + link cost that a module split does
  not remove. Slice 1's plausible (b) win is therefore bounded by roughly
  these 3 seconds, not the large win the sketch hoped for.
- Test-build delta lib-vs-leaf is ~1.4s (31.7 vs 30.3) on a ~30s floor.
- `cargo check` after a lib.rs edit is 11.8s — already tolerable.
- Implication: **slice 1 proceeds on its merge-safety goal** (lib.rs is the
  top conflict hotspot; 344/588 recent commits touch it), with build
  throughput as a secondary, now-bounded benefit. The slice-2 crate
  extractions keep their keep-only-if-measured-faster gate (signoff
  amendment 5), and these numbers predict that gate will bite: the V10
  comparison must beat the run-to-run spread shown above.
